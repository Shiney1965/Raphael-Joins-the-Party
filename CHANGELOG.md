# Changelog: Raphael Joins the Party

**1.0.0 is the first public release.** The builds below it were test builds that were never published; they are
listed so the release carries an honest record of how it got here.

---

## 1.0.0 (2026-10-27): first public release

- **His own conversation by clicking him.** Clicking Raphael now opens his own conversation, with his recorded lines,
  instead of the borrowed hireling menu. From it you can talk, send him to wait at camp, bring him back, or dismiss
  him for good. Saves that already had him recruited switch over on load.
- **Party banter on by default,** with a "Party banter" switch in MCM (Look tab).
- **"Prepare to uninstall" button** in MCM (Recruitment tab), with a confirmation. It refuses during combat. After it,
  the House of Hope does not offer him again in that save.
- **The test spells are gone,** including from saves made with a test build.
- The private (romance) topic in his conversation is not part of 1.0.
- Release name, version and description in the mod's metadata.

## 0.4b (test build): one Hotkeys place

- The empty "Hotkeys" tab on the mod's own MCM page was removed. The three hotkeys are set on MCM's own Hotkeys page.

## 0.4a (test build): optional traits

- **Infernal Charge:** Flames of Avernus now uses its own charge, regained on a long rest.
- **Four optional traits** on a new MCM Traits tab, all off by default: Soul Reaper (a killing blow gives back the
  Infernal Charge, once per short rest), Inevitable Resolve (once per short rest), Fiendish Blessing and Magic
  Resistance (the base game's effects).

## 0.3d to 0.3g (test builds): the House of Hope offer

- **The offer needs only the deal and the contract.** It now comes when you try to leave through the exit portal,
  whether or not you freed Hope or took something from the Archive.
- **Every refusal ends in a reckoning:** fight him (the base game's battle) or yield your soul (the base game's ending).
- **Freed Hope?** She stays free in her House when he joins.
- **Written player replies:** each box shows your character's reply for Yes and for No.
- **He arrives as in the base game,** with his infernal teleport, beside the portal.
- Fixed a crash on answering the offer (0.3e).

## 0.3a to 0.3c (test builds): his voice

- Combat barks and two-sided party banter with Karlach, Astarion, Gale, Lae'zel and Wyll, from his recorded lines.
- His own camp conversation, with cameras.

## 0.1 and 0.2 (test builds): the companion

- Raphael as a recruitable companion at the House of Hope: respec on recruitment, Infernal Heritage (Igniting Spark,
  Flames of Avernus, Infernal Guise, fire resistance, Heavy Armour proficiency), and Devil's Sight.
- His looks: Wardrobe, Signature, Devil form and Fiendish Ascension; support for other mods' Raphael forms.
- He steps aside at the Astral Plane and at the end of the game, and leaves for good if you destroy the contract.
- The optional Script Extender and MCM layer: settings, "Recruit anytime" mode and look hotkeys.
