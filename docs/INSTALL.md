# Install: Raphael Joins the Party

Raphael Joins the Party installs like a normal `.pak` mod. It works on its own; BG3 Script Extender and Mod
Configuration Menu (MCM) are optional and add its settings menu.

## Prerequisites

1. **Baldur's Gate 3**, Patch 8 or later.
2. **BG3 Mod Manager (BG3MM)**, recommended, or the game's own mod manager.
3. **Optional:** [BG3 Script Extender](https://github.com/Norbyte/bg3se) (version 29 or later) and
   [Mod Configuration Menu](https://www.nexusmods.com/baldursgate3/mods/9162). These give you the settings menu:
   "Recruit anytime" mode, the optional traits, the banter switch, look hotkeys, other mods' Raphael forms and the
   "Prepare to uninstall" button.

The mod can be added to a game already in progress.

## Install

1. **Unzip** `RaphaelJoinsTheParty-1.0.0.zip`. Inside you'll find `RaphaelJoinsTheParty.pak` and these documents.
2. **Copy the `.pak` into your BG3 Mods folder.** Open File Explorer, paste this into the address bar, and press Enter:
   ```
   %LocalAppData%\Larian Studios\Baldur's Gate 3\Mods
   ```
   Copy `RaphaelJoinsTheParty.pak` into that folder.
3. **Activate it.** In BG3 Mod Manager, refresh the mod list (`F5`). "Raphael Joins the Party" appears in the
   **Inactive** pane: drag it into the **Active** list. If you use MCM, keep **Mod Configuration Menu above** this mod.
   Click **Save Load Order to File**, then launch the game from BG3MM.
4. **Load your save.** He becomes available at the House of Hope in Act 3.

## Verify it loaded

- **In BG3 Mod Manager:** the mod is in the Active list, version 1.0.0.
- **With Script Extender:** the Script Extender console prints this line:
  ```
  [RJTP] Raphael Joins the Party SE layer 1.0.0 loaded (server).
  ```
- **With MCM:** MCM's menu has a "Raphael Joins the Party" page with Recruitment, Look and Traits tabs, and a
  "Raphael's forms" tab.

## How to recruit him

1. In Act 3, make the deal with Raphael and keep your contract (do not destroy or steal it).
2. Go to the House of Hope.
3. When you try to leave through the exit portal, he appears and makes his offer. Your character's replies are
   written in the box: **Yes** and **No** pick the reply shown for each.

If you refuse, he will not let you leave in peace: you fight him or yield your soul, as in the base game.

## Uninstall

**With MCM:** in a loaded save, outside combat, the host opens MCM → Raphael Joins the Party → Recruitment →
**Prepare to uninstall**, and confirms. He leaves the party; his own clothes are removed, and any items you gave him
go to one of your characters. A message confirms it. Then save, quit, and deactivate or delete the mod.

**Without MCM:** click him, choose to dismiss him for good, then save, quit and remove the mod. If he never joined
you, remove the mod only while you are outside the House of Hope.
